NG PVP ONE-LINE PLAYER LAUNCHER

The player does NOT enter a PIN. The Agent automatically registers with the NG PVP server and waits for staff.

PowerShell template (replace YOUR-HTTPS-DOMAIN with your real server):
powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -Command "$u='https://YOUR-HTTPS-DOMAIN/NGPVP.Agent.exe'; $p=Join-Path $env:TEMP 'NGPVP.Agent.exe'; Invoke-WebRequest $u -OutFile $p; Start-Process $p"

Staff sees connected players automatically and presses START PC CHECK on the selected player.
